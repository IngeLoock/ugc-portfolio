Questions

Good to know

What's the difference between UGC and influencer content?

UGC is made for your own channels, website and ads, and it isn't tied to my follower count. If you'd also like it shared with my audience, add a tagged post or a collab post to any package.

Will you post the content on your page too?

Only if you'd like me to. A tagged post on my feed is +$150 and a collab post, shared to your profile and mine, is +$300. Collab posts reach both audiences at once, and they're how my Ringers Western and Lorna Jane content reached 20.3K and 6,640 views. With a collab post, you approve the reel first (one round of revisions included), I write the caption, and we plan the posting time together with your team.

Who owns the content?

You receive a licence for the usage in your package, organic or paid, for a set period. Longer usage, raw footage and whitelisting are available as add ons and agreed in writing before the shoot.

Do you travel for resort and hotel shoots?

Yes. South East Queensland and Northern NSW are easy, and I travel interstate and overseas for stays. Accommodation and travel are covered by the property or brand.

How quickly will I receive my content?

Photos are delivered within one week of the shoot, and videos within one to two weeks. Need it sooner for a launch? 72 hour delivery is available as an add on.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit secures your shoot date, with the balance due on delivery. Pay your deposit online by card through Stripe, or ask for an invoice and pay by bank transfer. Your date is locked in as soon as the deposit lands.

Can you help with strategy too?

That's my day job. If your content needs a sharper message or a brand refresh behind it, I'll shape the concept around what your brand stands for, so every shoot builds the same story.

Can you help with my website?

Yes. I run Studio Loock, a Gold Coast creative studio for branding, websites and SEO. We design and build websites that look like the brand and turn visitors into bookings, from brand new builds to facelifts of an existing site. Pair it with a content shoot and your new site launches with imagery made for it.
